The Masnavī

Jalāl al-Dīn Rūmī · a translation & commentary

The Masnavī-yi Ma‘navī is composed in six books (daftars) of rhyming couplets — some twenty-five thousand verses of stories, teaching, and the longing of the reed for its reed-bed. Choose a book to begin.
